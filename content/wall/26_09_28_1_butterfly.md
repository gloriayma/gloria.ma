- Take care of my health
- Preserve my faith in the universe 
- Make sure I don't do local harm to the world

But I verbalized something a few minutes ago in the shower that felt right: 

I want to protect my butterfly ideas. *All ideas are butterfly ideas, I want to treat all ideas as butterfly ideas*. I hope to be aware of the slightest squashing, even of course incidental, of my butterfly ideas, so that I can signpost explicitly that I am nurturing a butterfly idea. I hope that I am met by a MATS community and my teammates and my mentors who will understand this even without my explicit signposting and telling them to read a fricking LessWrong post. (The idea of "butterfly ideas" I did get from an Aceso Under Glass, which is currently refusing to load, but it's a great post linked on my curius). I hope they understand that if I seem confused and yet still optimistic, it is because inside me I am harboring a butterfly of an idea. 

I have an idea for what my goal with is for my research at MATS, and my research in general - why I do research. This came to me in the shower; it is a butterfly idea; I will leave it to be verbalized a future day :)
